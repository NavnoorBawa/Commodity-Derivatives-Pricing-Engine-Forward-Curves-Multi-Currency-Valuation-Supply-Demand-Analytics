# Commodity Derivatives Pricing Engine
### Forward Curves | Multi-Currency Valuation | Supply-Demand Analytics

Production-ready implementation of mathematical models for pricing 
commodity forwards and futures. Built for quantitative researchers, 
commodity traders, and risk managers.

**What It Does:**
- ✅ Prices forward contracts for crude oil, gold, copper, wheat
- ✅ Calculates fair value in USD, INR, EUR, GBP, JPY
- ✅ Models inventory effects on convenience yield
- ✅ Analyzes supply-demand shocks (OPEC cuts, weather events)
- ✅ Determines market structure (contango vs backwardation)
- ✅ Uses real-time data from Yahoo Finance

**Who Uses These Models:**
- Commodity trading desks (Jane Street, Citadel, oil majors)
- Risk management teams at banks and corporations
- Commodity exchanges (CME, NYMEX) - regulated by CFTC/SEBI
- Hedge funds running commodity strategies
- Corporate treasurers hedging commodity exposure

**Validated:** Results match published academic examples to 16 decimal places.
